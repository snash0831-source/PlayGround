How to Play

The Objective

Three rival elements share the Nexus. Shatter their crystal towers while keeping yours alive. The last tower standing claims the arena.

Each nexus has 950 integrity. Towers fire defensive bolts at anything that comes close.

Controls

  • W A S DMove your avatar
  • MOUSEAim your element
  • LMB / SPACECast basic attack
  • SHIFTElemental dash (brief immunity)
  • EUltimate — when fully charged
  • WHEEL / ESCZoom · Pause

Elemental Doctrine

FireSplash and burn. Meteor Fall cracks clustered foes and towers.
WaterSlow and sustain. Maelstrom heals you and your nexus.
EarthHeavy impacts, bonus tower damage. Cataclysm stuns.
AirRapid twin bolts. Cyclone drags enemies into the vortex.

Damage fills your ultimate. Bank health by fighting near your own nexus. On touch devices, use the twin sticks: left to move, right to aim and cast.
